# 🧭 CurioScout: Play & Learn Academy

An interactive, responsive suite of educational mini games designed specifically for kids. Built with modern HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Web Audio API, and Web Speech API. **Zero external dependencies, 100% self-contained, and works completely offline!**

## 🤖 Built-In Scout AI Copilot & Engine

- **100% In-Browser & Private:** Zero API keys, zero tracking, works offline, and fully child-safe.
- **Neural Sketch Recognition:** Real-time geometric feature extraction (bounding box, aspect ratio, density, circularity, curvature) to classify kid drawings.
- **Curious Q&A Companion:** Tap the **`🤖 Scout AI`** button in the header to ask curious questions (*"Why is the sky blue?", "How do rockets fly?", "Why do cats purr?"*) or get real-time hints!
- **Dynamic Branching Narratives:** Adapts story chapters on the fly based on choices made by the child.

---

## ✨ Kid-Centric Features

- **Web Audio Sound Effects**: Cute synthesized pops, chimes, xylophone tones, and triumphant fanfare without requiring any external audio files.
- **Voice Narration (Web Speech API)**: Speaks questions, letters, animal names, and encouraging cheers aloud for pre-readers.
- **Age-Adaptive Modes**:
  - **Toddler (Ages 3–5)**: Visual counting (1–8), 3-letter words, simple shape/color sorting.
  - **Explorer (Ages 6–8)**: Addition/subtraction up to 20, 4–5 letter words, world habitats.
  - **Super Star (Ages 9+)**: Multiplication, 6–8 letter spelling, advanced mental math.
- **Sticker Book & Trophy Room**: Earn stars with every correct answer to unlock 12 collectible stickers and 8 achievement badges.
- **Parent & Educator Report**: Real-time dashboard tracking stars earned, domain progress, and high scores.
- **Responsive & Touch-Ready**: Optimized for tablets, touch screens, mobile devices, and desktop computers. Includes a one-click **Fullscreen Mode**.
